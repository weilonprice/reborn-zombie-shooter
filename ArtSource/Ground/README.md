# Arena ground

Replacement artwork: tan dirt, muted olive grass and gray stone patches.
Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Ground_Source.png is the
master; build_ground.py restores Assets/_Project/Art/Ground/Ground_Albedo.png.
Existing material and texture GUID retained. Texture repeats every 24 metres;
the floor remains flat and fully walkable.

Prompt: square seamless tileable albedo for a stylized low-poly top-down zombie
survival mobile game; warm tan packed dirt, irregular muted sage/olive grass
patches and warm gray rocky gravel; organically interlocking regions, worn dirt
paths, small angular pebbles, subtle short grass brush marks; hand-painted
simplified angular shapes, restrained detail, moderate low contrast, flat diffuse
lighting; no buildings, characters, text, border, grid or pavement.
