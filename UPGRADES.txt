REBORN - UPGRADES REFERENCE
Snapshot of what is actually IN the build. Not a design document - if it is
written here, it exists in code and has been compiled. Ideas that have been
discussed but not built are listed at the bottom, clearly separated.

Last updated: 2026-09-09, after the mod-core removal and the cost-curve
rework. Uncommitted at time of writing.
NOTHING IN THIS FILE HAS BEEN PLAY-TESTED.


===============================================================================
TWO SYSTEMS, BOTH LIVE
===============================================================================

The game currently has two separate ways to make a weapon stronger, and they
do not know about each other:

  1. WEAPON UPGRADE TREES - the new system. Data-driven, per weapon, three
     paths of five tiers under a 5-3-0 rule. Only the pistol has a tree, and
     only one of its three paths is authored.

  2. MOD CORES - the older system. A fixed enum of five global mods bought
     once and applied to every weapon whose tags match.

This overlap is deliberate for now but is not a resting state. Retiring mod
cores into trees is Tech Debt #15, and Tech Debt #17 covers the flat flag
vocabulary the trees use. Both should be done as one rework, not two.

Order of application matters: upgrades set the base magazine, mods then scale
it. So Extended Drum Mags multiplies the UPGRADED size, not the printed one.
That single source of truth lives in ArmoryManager.EffectiveMagazineSize, and
it has to stay there - computing it twice is what caused the bug where the
Armory topped an upgraded rifle to 30 rounds while it held 45.


===============================================================================
SYSTEM 1 - WEAPON UPGRADE TREES
===============================================================================

Bought with gold at the Armory between waves, via the WEAPON UPGRADES button.
Gold resets each run, so trees reset with it.

TIER COSTS
  T1 60   T2 150   T3 350   T4 800   T5 2000

  One curve, shared by every path on every weapon, defined once as
  ArenaBuilder.TierCosts and applied by the UpgradePath helper. Authoring a
  path is choosing five effects; costs are not a per-path decision.

  Full path 3360. Dip to T3 560. Under 5-3-0 one weapon can absorb 3920.

  The curve is steep on purpose. Measured 2026-09-09: a 15-wave run earns
  6580 gold (338 enemies at 18.7 average, plus 250 from the boss), and under
  the previous flat curve the maximum possible spend across all four weapons
  was 6870 - so a run could buy 96% of everything the game sells. Nothing was
  a choice; buying was just sequencing. It also lands the expensive tiers
  late, where the money actually is: 55% of a run's gold arrives in the last
  five waves.


THE 5-3-0 RULE
  One path may reach tier 5.
  A second path may reach tier 3.
  The third is locked at 0 and can never be started.

  Enforced in UpgradeManager.IsNextTierAllowed, which evaluates the rule
  against the configuration a purchase WOULD produce rather than the one it
  starts from - the rule is about the result, not the starting point.

  The panel draws "locked by 5-3-0" and "you cannot afford this yet" as
  visibly different states. Greying both identically makes the rule read as
  arbitrary rather than as a consequence of a choice already made.

WHICH WEAPONS HAVE TREES
  Pistol     - one authored path, two placeholder columns
  Shotgun    - none
  Rifle      - none
  Sniper     - none

  A weapon with no tree runs on its definition values plus mod cores, and
  its panel reads "This weapon has no upgrade paths yet."


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PISTOL - PATH 1 OF 3: "GUNSLINGER"
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Summary: open hot, close hot, and eventually stop needing to aim at all.
Full path costs 3360 gold. See TIER COSTS below.

Pistol base for reference: 20 damage, 200 RPM, 12 magazine, 120 reserve,
1.0s reload, 1.2 degree spread, semi-automatic.

T1  QUICK DRAW                                                       60 gold
    Fire rate 200 -> 320. Reload 1.0s -> 0.75s.
    The first shot after every reload is a guaranteed crit at 2x damage.
    Arms only on a reload that actually loaded rounds, so a dry reserve
    cannot hand out a free crit.

T2  DEADEYE                                                         150 gold
    Damage 20 -> 30. Fire rate -> 380. Reload -> 0.6s.
    25% chance any shot crits for double damage.
T3  FAN THE HAMMER                                                  350 gold
    Magazine 12 -> 18. Crit chance -> 35%.
    Holding the trigger past 0.2s fires at 600 RPM while spread degrades
    from 1.2 to 9 degrees over one second, recovering over 0.4s on release.
    A CLICK IS STILL AN ACCURATE SINGLE SHOT. The 0.2s threshold is what
    keeps the accurate shot alive; it is the number most likely to need
    tuning in the whole path.

T4  TRUE GUNSLINGER                                                 800 gold
    Damage 30 -> 45. Magazine -> 30. Reload -> 0.45s. Fan rate -> 750 RPM.
    DUAL PISTOLS: a second gun appears, shots alternate hands, both guns
    keep the player's forward so only the muzzle origin moves.
    INFINITE RESERVE: reloads still happen, the reserve never depletes.

T5  LEGEND OF THE WEST                                             2000 gold
    Crit chance -> 45%.
    ULTIMATE, charged by 30 pistol kills, fired with [V]:
      - flourish reload that does not touch the reserve
      - PlayerController takes over facing; the player spins at 540 deg/s
      - mouse and right stick do nothing; WASD still works
      - guns auto-aim the nearest enemy within 22m, one target per shot
      - 80% crit chance for the duration
      - 900 RPM, and the ability ends when the magazine runs dry
      - opens on 0.35s of slow motion at 0.35x
    Duration is therefore 30 rounds / 15 per second = 2.0 SECONDS.
    That may be short for something costing 30 kills. Raise the T5 magazine
    or lower the ultimate fire rate if it is.

    Charge rules: only kills by this weapon count, barrels and claymores do
    not. Kills scored DURING the ultimate do not pay for the next one.
    Charge survives weapon swaps; the ability itself does not.
    The ability ends itself on death or when the wave break starts, because
    Weapon stops updating in both cases and would otherwise never reach an
    empty magazine, stranding the player spinning with no way out.

SHOTS TO KILL A STANDARD ZOMBIE (100 HP), by tier:
    base  5      T1  5  (4 opening with the crit)
    T2    4  (3) T3  4  (3)      T4  3  (2)
    Brute is 300 HP: 7 shots at T4. Boss is 2000 HP.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PISTOL - PATHS 2 AND 3: NOT DESIGNED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Both render in the panel as "- TO BE DESIGNED -" with no tiers, so every
button in those columns is inert.

Until they exist the 5-3-0 rule has nothing to bite on. One path is a ladder,
not a build - there is currently no choice to make, only an affordability
question.


===============================================================================
SYSTEM 2 - MOD CORES: REMOVED 2026-09-09
===============================================================================

Five global mods (Dragon's Breath, Heavy Slug, Bore Piercing, Extended Drum
Mags, Overclocked Receiver) were bought once at the Armory and applied to
every weapon whose tags matched. Deleted outright once upgrade trees landed -
two systems making a weapon stronger, neither aware of the other, with
untested stacking between them. This closed Tech Debt #15 by deletion rather
than by migrating them into trees.

Gone with them: ModCoreType, the five prices, the shop's right-hand column,
and WeaponDefinition's OverclockedFireRateMultiplier and
OverclockedConvertsToAuto. WeaponTags survives but is now unread - it was how
mods chose their weapons, and it is kept only as a likely hook for tiers that
target a weapon family.

===============================================================================
HOW TO ADD TO THIS
===============================================================================

A new TIER or a whole new PATH is pure data: add an UpgradeTier to the
authoring in ArenaBuilder.LoadOrCreatePistolUpgrades and re-run the builder.
No code.

A new KIND of effect is not. It costs a field on UpgradeTier, a member on
WeaponStats, a line in UpgradeManager.Apply, and a branch in the firing path.
UpgradeTier currently carries 18 behaviour fields for this reason. That is
Tech Debt #17, deliberately unfixed - designing the abstraction against a
single authored path would be guessing.

The tier data asset (Assets/_Project/Upgrades/UPG_Pistol.asset) is generated
by the builder under a create-if-missing contract: it is written once and
never overwritten, so hand-tuning survives a rebuild. The consequence is that
CHANGING AUTHORED TIERS IN CODE REQUIRES DELETING THE ASSET FIRST, or the
builder will keep the stale version with no error. This has already caused
one near-miss where a regenerated asset held a design we had replaced.


===============================================================================
DESIGNED BUT NOT BUILT
===============================================================================

Kept short on purpose - this file documents what exists. These are candidates
for the pistol's two empty paths, brainstormed 2026-09-09 and not started:

  Lawman      Fortification. Repair barricades by shooting them, cripple,
              stand-still damage bonus, barricade turrets. The only concept
              that makes deployables matter to a weapon.
  Reaper      Sustain. Life-steal on kill, damage scaling with missing
              health, one revive per wave. Blocked on a real question: there
              is NO healing anywhere in the game, so this would not be a
              playstyle, it would be the only sustain that exists.
  Marksman    Elites. Bonus damage to high-HP enemies, ramping damage on a
              single target, infinite pierce, one charged shot per magazine.
              Cheapest to build; answers the boss, which Gunslinger does not.
  Powder Keg  Crowds. Splash, dynamite every sixth round, reload throws a
              stick, corpses chain-detonate.
  Drifter     Mobility. Dash with i-frames, burning trail, blink through
              enemies. Adds a movement verb to the game, which is a decision
              about the game rather than about the gun.
  Undertaker  Chain death. Stacking poison that spreads on death, and a
              summon at T5.
  Wildfire    Fire. Ignite rather than kill, burning spreads through packs,
              ground fire, corpses explode, and a capstone that scales with
              how many enemies are alight. Weak early, apocalyptic late, and
              bad against the boss unless burn is allowed to stack on single
              targets.
  Hexslinger  Multi-element with reactions - shock a burning enemy to
              detonate it, and so on. Judged the most interesting and the
              most expensive, and a better fit for the rifle or shotgun than
              for a precision sidearm.

Every one of these needs new code. None of them are data-only.
