REBORN - UPGRADES REFERENCE
Snapshot of what is actually IN the build. Not a design document - if it is
written here, it exists in code and has been compiled. Ideas that have been
discussed but not built are listed at the bottom, clearly separated.

Last updated: 2026-09-10. Weapon roster complete at ten, one path each.

EVERYTHING HERE IS SUBJECT TO CHANGE, AND NONE OF IT HAS BEEN PLAY-TESTED.
Numbers in particular are first drafts. The tiers were designed against the
arithmetic (shots to kill, DPS, run income) rather than against how any of it
feels, and the arithmetic is the weaker of the two. Expect the play-test to
move things.

Where a number is a known risk it says so in the tier that owns it, rather
than in a list here - the warning belongs next to the thing it is about.


===============================================================================
THE SHAPE OF IT
===============================================================================

Every weapon has ONE upgrade path of five tiers, bought with gold at the
Armory between waves. The run's decision is which weapon to pour gold into: a
maxed path costs 3360 against 6580 of run income, so a run can only ever max
one. The player carries all ten weapons; exactly one of them feels powerful.

That is the third structure this has had. Three paths per weapon under a 5-3-0
rule came first, and needed all three authored before any of them was a
choice - 60 tiers before the design could be judged. The variety now comes
from the arsenal instead.

5-3-0 IS STILL IN THE CODE AND IS INERT.
  UpgradeManager still enforces "one path to 5, a second to 3, the third
  locked", but a single open path can never breach it, so it never triggers.
  Giving one weapon three paths later is data plus a wider panel - the rule,
  the resolver and the purchase flow all already handle it.


TIER COSTS
  T1 60   T2 150   T3 350   T4 800   T5 2000

  One curve, shared by every path on every weapon, defined once as
  ArenaBuilder.TierCosts and applied by the UpgradePath helper. Tiers carry no
  cost field at all - authoring a path is choosing five effects.

  Full path 3360.

  The curve is steep on purpose. Measured 2026-09-09: a 15-wave run earns
  6580 gold (338 enemies at 18.7 average, plus 250 from the boss). Under the
  previous flat curve a run could buy 96% of everything the game sells, so
  nothing was a choice. It also lands the expensive tiers late, where the
  money actually is: 55% of a run's gold arrives in the last five waves.


THE LOADOUT
  Ten carried slots on the number row, 1-9 then 0. WeaponLoadout holds a
  catalogue (everything the Armory sells) and a carried array (what the run
  holds). Weapon prices live on WeaponDefinition, so a new weapon is an asset
  plus a catalogue entry - no constant, no branch, no button.

  All ten weapons cost 2200 together. With a maxed path at 3360 that leaves
  roughly 1000 for ammo crates and deployables, so a run wanting a real
  barricade line has to skip a couple of guns.

  KNOWN GAP: the gamepad d-pad reaches the first four slots only. See debt 18.


MOD CORES: REMOVED 2026-09-09
  Five global mods (Dragon's Breath, Heavy Slug, Bore Piercing, Extended Drum
  Mags, Overclocked Receiver) were bought once and applied by weapon tag.
  Deleted outright once upgrade trees landed - two systems making a weapon
  stronger, neither aware of the other, with untested stacking between them.
  Closed Tech Debt #15 by deletion rather than by migration. WeaponTags
  survives but is unread; it is kept as a hook for tiers that might one day
  target a weapon family.


===============================================================================
FIRING MODES
===============================================================================

How a shot reaches the world is a WeaponDelivery asset on the definition, not
a branch in Weapon.cs. A weapon that leaves the field empty is an ordinary
hitscan line, which is what all four current weapons are.

The split matters when adding anything:

  WEAPON owns what is true of every firearm - ammo, rate of fire, which hand
  fired, whether the shot crit, which direction the ultimate picked, plus
  focus stacking, executions and kill credit.

  DELIVERY owns only how the shot travels. Everything it needs arrives in a
  ShotContext rather than being reached for through the weapon.

The consequence is that akimbo, the ultimate, crits, focus and executions all
work with every mode without a mode knowing they exist.

  HITSCAN     An instant line, split into pellets, punching through bodies
              until pierce runs out. Pistol, shotgun, rifle, sniper.
  CONE        A short arc damaging everything inside it. Continuous fire is
              modelled as a very high rate of very small shots, so a fuel tank
              is a magazine and a tick of flame is a round - rather than a
              second firing lifecycle running beside the first. Stopped by
              level geometry but NOT by barricades, matching bullets.
  CHAIN       Hits one body then hops to nearby ones. The first link is a real
              raycast so walls block it and aiming matters; every hop after is
              a search, because an arc refusing to bend around a corpse would
              read as broken rather than physical.
  PROJECTILE  Travel time, spherecast swept so a fast round cannot tunnel,
              statically pooled. Carries its damage rather than asking the
              weapon at impact - by the time it lands the player may have
              swapped weapons, bought a tier or died, and the shot has to be
              worth what it was worth when it was fired.
              Consequence: its kills charge no ultimate and refund no reserve.
              See debt 20.

Delivery PARAMETERS are upgradeable. Cone angle, chain hops, hop range, hop
damage, blast radius and range all live on WeaponStats as -1-means-default
overrides, so the asset says where a weapon starts and its path says where it
goes. Without that a Wildfire path could not widen its cone and a Storm path
could not grow its chain, which is what those paths are entirely about.

CONE, CHAIN AND PROJECTILE HAVE NEVER BEEN PLAYED. The flamethrower, Tesla
coil and grenade launcher are the first things to run them, so expect MODE
bugs before balance problems.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PISTOL - "GUNSLINGER"
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Summary: open hot, close hot, and eventually stop needing to aim at all.

Pistol base for reference: 20 damage, 200 RPM, 12 magazine, 120 reserve,
1.0s reload, 1.2 degree spread, semi-automatic.

T1  QUICK DRAW                                                       60 gold
    Fire rate 200 -> 320. Reload 1.0s -> 0.75s.
    The first shot after every reload is a guaranteed crit at 2x damage.
    Arms only on a reload that actually loaded rounds, so a dry reserve
    cannot hand out a free crit.

T2  DEADEYE                                                         150 gold
    Damage 20 -> 30. Fire rate -> 380. Reload -> 0.6s.
    25% chance any shot crits for double damage.
T3  FAN THE HAMMER                                                  350 gold
    Magazine 12 -> 18. Crit chance -> 35%.
    Holding the trigger past 0.2s fires at 600 RPM while spread degrades
    from 1.2 to 9 degrees over one second, recovering over 0.4s on release.
    A CLICK IS STILL AN ACCURATE SINGLE SHOT. The 0.2s threshold is what
    keeps the accurate shot alive; it is the number most likely to need
    tuning in the whole path.

T4  TRUE GUNSLINGER                                                 800 gold
    Damage 30 -> 45. Magazine -> 30. Reload -> 0.45s. Fan rate -> 750 RPM.
    DUAL PISTOLS: a second gun appears, shots alternate hands, both guns
    keep the player's forward so only the muzzle origin moves.
    INFINITE RESERVE: reloads still happen, the reserve never depletes.

T5  LEGEND OF THE WEST                                             2000 gold
    Crit chance -> 45%.
    ULTIMATE, charged by 30 pistol kills, fired with [V]:
      - flourish reload that does not touch the reserve
      - PlayerController takes over facing; the player spins at 540 deg/s
      - mouse and right stick do nothing; WASD still works
      - guns auto-aim the nearest enemy within 22m, one target per shot
      - 80% crit chance for the duration
      - 900 RPM, and the ability ends when the magazine runs dry
      - opens on 0.35s of slow motion at 0.35x
    Duration is therefore 30 rounds / 15 per second = 2.0 SECONDS.
    That may be short for something costing 30 kills. Raise the T5 magazine
    or lower the ultimate fire rate if it is.

    Charge rules: only kills by this weapon count, barrels and claymores do
    not. Kills scored DURING the ultimate do not pay for the next one.
    Charge survives weapon swaps; the ability itself does not.
    The ability ends itself on death or when the wave break starts, because
    Weapon stops updating in both cases and would otherwise never reach an
    empty magazine, stranding the player spinning with no way out.

SHOTS TO KILL A STANDARD ZOMBIE (100 HP), by tier:
    base  5      T1  5  (4 opening with the crit)
    T2    4  (3) T3  4  (3)      T4  3  (2)
    Brute is 300 HP: 7 shots at T4. Boss is 2000 HP.

SHOTGUN - "STREETSWEEPER"
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Summary: stops being a gun and becomes a wall of lead that moves the horde.
Base: 22 dmg x 5 pellets, 75 RPM, 8 shells, 48 reserve, 2.4s reload, 7 spread.

T1  CHOKE                                                            60 gold
    7 pellets instead of 5, spread 7 to 5.5 degrees.
T2  SPEEDLOADER                                                     150 gold
    Reload 2.4s to 1.2s, magazine 8 to 10.
T3  AUTO LOADER                                                     350 gold
    Hold to fire, 75 to 160 RPM.
T4  RIOT GUN                                                        800 gold
    10 pellets at 26 damage, knockback 2.2 to 4.
T5  STREETSWEEPER                                                  2000 gold
    12 pellets, 200 RPM, 20 shells, 0.9s reload, spread out to 9 degrees.
    Every kill refunds one shell to the reserve, which is the only reason it
    can sustain that rate - 48 shells would otherwise last about 25 seconds.
    In a crowd it runs a net surplus; in a sparse wave it still drains.

ASSAULT RIFLE - "MARKSMAN"
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Summary: rewards staying on one target - the opposite of spraying a crowd.
Base: 22 dmg, 600 RPM, 30 mag, 180 reserve, 1.7s reload, 2.2 spread, pierce 2.

T1  MATCH BARREL                                                     60 gold
    Damage 22 to 25, spread 2.2 to 1.2.
T2  FOCUS FIRE                                                      150 gold
    Consecutive hits on the SAME enemy add 4% damage each, up to 40%.
    Hitting anything else starts over. A stack expires 1.5s after its last hit.
T3  STABILISED                                                      350 gold
    Holding the trigger tightens spread to 0.4 instead of widening it.
    Magazine 30 to 45.
T4  ARMOUR BREAKER                                                  800 gold
    Damage to 32, focus ceiling to 100%, full damage through pierced bodies.
T5  MARKSMAN                                                       2000 gold
    Focus ceiling 200%, 700 RPM, pierce 4. A held burst ends up hitting three
    times as hard as it started. Roughly 1100 single-target DPS once ramped,
    and worth nothing at all against a crowd.

    NOTE: focus is counted per HIT, not per shot. Correct only while the
    weapons that have it fire one pellet - a multi-pellet weapon with focus
    would stack it once per pellet.

SNIPER RIFLE - "EXECUTIONER"
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Summary: one round, one line, and everything standing in it.
Base: 150 dmg, 45 RPM, 5 mag, 30 reserve, 2.8s reload, pierce 6, falloff 0.85.

T1  MATCH ROUNDS                                                     60 gold
    Damage 150 to 190, falloff to 1.0 - the last enemy in a line takes as
    much as the first.
T2  BREACH                                                          150 gold
    Pierce 6 to 10, magazine 5 to 7.
T3  CYCLED BOLT                                                     350 gold
    Fire rate 45 to 75, reload 2.8s to 1.8s.
T4  OVERPENETRATION                                                 800 gold
    Damage 220, and each body pierced makes the round 20% STRONGER.
T5  EXECUTIONER                                                    2000 gold
    300 damage, pierce 12, 20% gain per body, and anything at or under 30%
    health dies outright whatever its maximum.

    TWO NUMBERS TO WATCH. The gain compounds: 1.2 over 12 bodies is roughly
    9x on the last one, so a perfectly lined-up corridor shot deals north of
    10,000 total. And the execute applies to the boss, deleting its last 600
    HP for free - that is the promise of the weapon, but it is a big one.

FLAMETHROWER - "WILDFIRE"                                         250 gold
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Summary: stops being a weapon you aim and becomes a shape you pour over them.
Base: 7 dmg x 10 ticks/sec, 26 degree cone, 11m, 100 fuel, 400 reserve, 2.2s.
Delivery: DLV_Flame (cone). Stopped by level geometry, NOT by barricades.

T1  PRESSURISED           60    Reach 11m to 15m, 7 to 9 damage a tick.
T2  WIDE BURNER          150    Cone 26 to 38 degrees. A wall, not a jet.
T3  BACKPACK TANK        350    Fuel 100 to 200, reload 2.2s to 1.4s.
T4  WHITE HEAT           800    9 to 16 a tick, 600 to 750 ticks a minute.
T5  FIRESTORM           2000    50 degree cone, 18m, 22 a tick, endless fuel.

    IT IS SILENT ON PURPOSE. Ten shots a second machine-guns the shared
    gunshot clip through the voice pool and reads as a stutter. Needs a
    looping sample - see debt 22. It will feel wrong before then.
    NO BURN OR SPREAD YET. The ignite-and-spread fantasy from the original
    Wildfire sketch needs a status subsystem; this path is reach, width, fuel
    and heat. Honest, playable, and not the whole idea.

TESLA COIL - "STORM"                                              300 gold
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Summary: one shot, and everything standing near it.
Base: 34 dmg, 150 RPM, 16 mag, 96 reserve, 2.0s reload, 30m.
Delivery: DLV_Tesla (chain), 3 hops at 8m carrying 80% each.

T1  CAPACITOR             60    Damage 34 to 44 on the body you actually hit.
T2  ARC EXTENDER         150    5 hops instead of 3, 10m between them.
T3  SUPERCONDUCTOR       350    95% carried per hop instead of 80%. Mag 24.
T4  OVERCHARGE           800    Damage 52, 150 to 240 RPM, reload 1.3s.
T5  STORM               2000    8 hops, 13m reach, and the arc GAINS 10% per
                                hop. The back of a tight pack is the worst
                                place in the arena.

    WATCH T5. Ten percent over eight hops means the last body takes over twice
    the first - roughly 690 damage per shot through a perfect nine-pack. Same
    compounding shape as sniper Overpenetration, same first tuning candidate.

SMG - "ADRENALINE"                                                120 gold
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Summary: momentum you have to keep earning. Stop killing and it drains away.
Base: 14 dmg, 900 RPM, 40 mag, 320 reserve, 1.5s reload, 3.4 spread.

T1  HAIR TRIGGER          60    1050 RPM, spread 3.4 to 2.6.
T2  ADRENALINE           150    Kills stack 4% move speed AND 4% fire rate, to
                                eight. A stack lasts 4s without another kill.
T3  DEEP MAG             350    Damage 19, magazine 60, reload 1.1s.
T4  OVERDRIVE            800    Stacks to fifteen, holding six seconds.
T5  REDLINE             2000    Damage 28, 5% per stack to twenty - double
                                speed and double rate while the chain lives.
                                Reserve never runs dry.

    Stacks lapse on a TIMER, not on standing still. A velocity check would
    punish backing off round a corner, which is what the weapon wants you
    doing. It is also the only weapon that touches player move speed.

NAIL GUN - "LAWMAN"                                               180 gold
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Summary: the only weapon that would rather you were shooting your own wall.
Base: 26 dmg, 260 RPM, 24 mag, 200 reserve, 1.6s reload. HITSCAN - see below.

T1  FIELD REPAIR          60    Nails in your own barricade mend it 22 a hit
                                instead of passing through.
T2  PINNING SHOT         150    Hits hold an enemy still 0.4s. Damage 32.
T3  FRAMING NAILER       350    400 RPM, magazine 40, repair 35.
T4  REBAR                800    Damage 45, pins 0.8s, repair 50.
T5  LAWMAN              2000    Every barricade you place mounts a turret
                                firing this weapon at a third its rate.
                                Damage 60, repair 70, endless reserve.

    HITSCAN ON PURPOSE, and it is mechanical rather than flavour.
    WeaponProjectile carries its own damage and never calls Weapon.ApplyShot,
    where repair and pinning live - a projectile nail gun would have had a
    completely inert path. See debt 20 before giving any weapon a projectile
    delivery if its upgrades do anything on hit beyond damage.

    The turret sits dormant on every barricade prefab and switches itself on
    while a carried weapon grants it, so DeployablePlacer never learns that
    upgrades exist.

SIPHON RIFLE - "REAPER"                                           380 gold
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Summary: the only healing in the game, and it only pays while you are winning.
Base: 40 dmg, 260 RPM, 20 mag, 140 reserve, 1.9s reload, pierce 1.

T1  BLOOD MONEY           60    Kills restore 2 health.
T2  DEATHWISH            150    Up to +50% damage as health falls.
T3  HARVEST              350    Kills restore 5. Damage 52, magazine 30.
T4  PALE HORSE           800    Up to +120% at the edge of death, kills
                                restore 8.
T5  SECOND LIFE         2000    Once a wave a killing blow leaves you at 1
                                health - where you also hit hardest. Damage
                                70, kills restore 12.

    Damage scales with health LOST rather than remaining, so it pays out
    exactly when the lifesteal is most needed.
    T5 is a death INTERCEPTOR, not a revive: no death ever happens, so the
    run-over screen, the score tally and the wave loop never see it. The hook
    lives on Health, so enemies and the player share it.


===============================================================================
IDEAS NOT ON THE ROSTER
===============================================================================

Brainstormed 2026-09-09 and not selected. Kept because the reasoning may be
worth reusing when a weapon needs an identity:

  Lawman, Reaper, Marksman, Wildfire and Storm all became roster weapons.
  Powder Keg   Crowds via explosives - dynamite every sixth round, reload
               lobs a stick, corpses chain-detonate. Folded into Bombardier.
  Drifter      Mobility with a dash and i-frames. Rejected as a pistol path
               because it adds a movement VERB to the game, which is a
               decision about the game rather than about a gun. Partly
               survives as the SMG's Adrenaline.
  Undertaker   Stacking poison that spreads on death, with a summon at T5.
               The most memorable capstone brainstormed and the second most
               expensive. Still unclaimed.
  Hexslinger   Multi-element with reactions - shock a burning enemy to
               detonate it, freeze then shatter. Judged the most interesting
               design and the most expensive by a wide margin. A better fit
               for a rifle or shotgun than a precision sidearm, and it wants
               the firing-mode work done first.
